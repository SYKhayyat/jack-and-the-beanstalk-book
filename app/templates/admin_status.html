The review loop

Every picture is checked by tier 1 (free: blank, wrong shape, a grid of tiles, the plate redrawn) and then by the reviewer. A decision model looks first and settles the pictures it is sure about; the unsure ones go to the reviewer. A picture neither could judge is kept on tier 1 alone and counted here.
