What we keep, and for how long

  • The photo you upload is used to draw the star's portrait. An unfinished draft is deleted after {{ draft_retention_hours }} hours.
  • A finished book (its pages, the portrait and the photo it was drawn from) is kept for {{ book_retention_days }} days after it was last changed, then deleted.
  • A remembered child is kept only if you ticked “remember”, and only until you forget it or {{ child_profile_retention_days }} days pass without a book made from it.
  • A print order keeps the shipping address it was sent to, so a lost parcel can be traced.
  • Your wallet history — top-ups, charges and refunds — is a financial record. It is filed under your Google account's id, not your name or email, and it stays after an account is deleted.
  • From Google we keep your name, email and picture, to show who is signed in.

Photos and pictures are sent to the image service that draws them and to the reviewer that checks them, and to the printer when you order a copy. They are not used for anything else, and nothing is sold.
