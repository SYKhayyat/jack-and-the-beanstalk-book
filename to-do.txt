3. [x] Ability to see all books when you sign in.
       Google mode shows a library sidebar of the signed-in person's books
       (newest first, cover thumbnails, status). Each book opens at
       /books/{id} with the PDF, every page, and the edit controls: redraw one
       page, replace a face, redraw the cover, rewrite the About text, delete.
       Password mode deliberately has no library (no person to file books
       under).
       The "it says it has run out, makes me log in again, and wipes my book"
       part was two things: Google's Testing-mode consent screen expires
       sessions after 7 days (publish the consent screen — see
       docs/google-oauth-setup.md), and a session expiring mid-render. The
       second is fixed in code: the book, its pages, its PDF, its portrait and
       the original photo are stored per user, an expired session sends the
       visitor through sign-in back to the same book (/login?next=/books/{id}),
       and the library is the way back if the tab was closed.
